# Tell — Charter

Return to this when deciding what to build, change or cut. A change that can't be justified
here doesn't ship. `AGENTS.md` turns it into rules the code must keep; `DECISIONS.md` records why.

## Vision

People understand their own judgement and keep the freedom to question and shape it, as
technology increasingly influences what they notice, believe and choose.

## Mission

Make self-discovery enjoyable through short games and a growing record people can inspect,
challenge and use on their own terms.

## Promise

A few minutes of play. A clearer view of how you see.

## Core job

A worthwhile way to spend a spare moment that leaves something lasting: nothing to memorise,
nothing lost.

## Principles

1. **Enjoyment before profiling.** Every round is worth playing on its own.
2. **Evidence before interpretation.** Show what happened, with its denominator, before
   suggesting what it means.
3. **Transparent rules.** Every count, call-out and deal follows a rule the player can read in
   the app.
4. **The player has the last word.** People can qualify, reject and revise any interpretation.
   Their view is a note, never proof.
5. **Open perspectives.** Offer rival explanations and exceptions. Never reinforce a fixed
   identity, and never treat cynicism as depth.
6. **Light accumulation.** The record grows by itself. Nothing to memorise, nothing lost, no
   backlog.
7. **Agency over engagement.** Short sessions with a clear end, no streaks or penalties, and the
   player's data under the player's control.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Answers are recorded facts. Interpretations are authored hypotheses, and say so.
- No pattern without a published rule. Tell never manufactures patterns or diagnoses biases.
- Comparisons only between comparable scenes, always with explicit denominators.
- An angle you rarely pick is not a blind spot. A changed answer is not a diagnosis.
- Approval is never proof, and approving one thing never approves what is derived from it.
- Invented scenes are labelled. Real events, if ever used, carry sources and dates.
- No runtime AI, accounts, analytics or third-party network. Data stays on the device unless
  the player exports it.

## The feature test

> Does this make exploration more enjoyable or self-understanding more grounded, while
> preserving the player's freedom and keeping the experience light?

## Success

People come back without being reminded, and their observations stay credible when they inspect
the evidence.

## Scope

**Now (v1):** a phone PWA with Play and Map for two areas (The world; Work & projects), three
kinds of round (Decode, Mirror, paired choices), and local storage with export and restore.

**Later, only if v1 earns it:** see `FUTURE.md`. Decide (briefs for your own AI) is the main
candidate; it must draw only on entries the player has approved and lets them edit.
