# Pilot plan

A short manual pilot of what matters more than any technical check: whether the stories are worth
playing by themselves, and whether the Map holds something worth remembering. There are no
analytics: evidence comes from observation, what pilots say and, with their consent, the plain-
text download they choose to send (About → "Download as plain text").

**Who:** five or six people, including the owner, who haven't seen Tell's content. **How long:**
one observed session, a week of their own, then a short conversation. **Setup:** each installs
Tell on their own phone and is told only "Short stories to play. Your map keeps what turns up."
No reminders during the week.

## 1. Is play worth it by itself?

- **Watch** the first session, thinking aloud, without help. Where do they slow down, smile or
  hesitate over a choice? Where do they tap through? Do they open "What's real in this story"?
- **Ask after two stories, before the third:** "What do you think the next one will ask you to
  do?" Then after it: was it what you expected?
- **Ask at the end:** Which would you play again, and why? Did any feel like it had a right
  answer? Did asking or looking ever feel like it gave the answer away?
- **Holds if:** most can't predict the third story's controls or turn, most play four of six in
  the first sitting unprompted, and nobody reports a right answer or a give-away.
- **Fails if:** pilots predict the shape, skim the endings, or name an answer the app wanted.

## 2. Does the Map hold something worth remembering?

- **Watch** the first time a connection appears on an ending. Is it read? Is the disclosure
  opened? Does the "New" label register without feeling like a reward?
- **Ask straight away:** in your own words, what's the distinction? Does it fit what you actually
  chose, or feel like it's about someone else's choice?
- **A day or more later, without the app:** which connection do you remember? Has its question
  come up for anything real? (Write down what they say, not whether it was right.)
- **After a replay down another path:** did the Map change in a way that made sense? Did anything
  you'd seen before look new, or disappear?
- **Holds if:** most restate one distinction a day later, at least some name a real choice it
  touched, and nobody says a connection misdescribed their choice.
- **Fails if:** connections read as obvious, as verdicts on the player, or as not matching the
  path they took.

## Afterwards

Write what happened as a dated entry in `DECISIONS.md`: what held, what failed, what changes.
Stories named predictable or pointless are rewritten or retired; connections that add nothing
beyond the two choices, or misdescribe a path, are cut. Both follow the review rule in
`AGENTS.md`.
